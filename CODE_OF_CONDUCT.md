# Code of Conduct

This community follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcoming and constructive. Harassment, insults, personal attacks and
publishing other people's private information are not tolerated here.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments and issues that break these rules, and may block
people who repeatedly do so. To report a problem, email **support@makeprog.com** with
"Code of Conduct" in the subject; reports are handled confidentially.
